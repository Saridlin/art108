Doodle and Twiddle is a cute 2D platformer where the player controls a small bug called Doodle, and is trying to survive in a post-apocalyptic world to meet up with their lost friend, Twiddle. 

![Image]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/vg-prototype.png)
> Play the most recent Alpha Build of *Doodle and Twiddle* [here](https://saridlin.itch.io/doodle-n-twiddle)!

### How to Play
**A** to move Left, **D** to move Right\
**Spacebar** to Jump\
**O** to leave Trash Pile\
**ESC** to exit out of Settings menu

The player's goal is to reach the trash pile at the top of the screen successfully and avoid any hazards in the post-apocalyptic landscape!

### Current Known Issues:
- When player character exits the Trash Pile, exit animation does not play
- Trash Pile exit animation can be activated however, by pressing “O” when the player character has not entered it
- No death animation/visual indicator for when player character “dies” on the spike hazard

### Game Development
This last prototype version of the game contains the bare-bones architecture for the game. Players can move around the world, interact with hazards and a possible finish goal, as well as, find the controls information for the game as well. The sprite animations for the player character play well, and the colliding boxes on the level’s platforms are executed correctly.\
I hope to continue this game and increase the level size/platform number so that there is more for the player to explore around with. I would also want to incorporate the proper intended finish goal where the player finds their other bug friend in the level.
